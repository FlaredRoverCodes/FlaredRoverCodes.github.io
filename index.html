about

I'm a backend engineer based in Seattle, currently focused on distributed data systems. Over the past seven years I've moved between startups and larger platforms, usually landing on the team responsible for the thing everything else depends on: the queue, the cache layer, the service that can't go down.

I care most about systems that fail predictably — clear error states, honest logs, and dashboards a tired on-call engineer can actually read. Outside of work I maintain a couple of small open-source tools and I'm slowly working through a personal project to self-host everything I can.
